04 / About

Two decades behind Mumbai's cameras.

Just Video Broadcast started in 2002 serving the broadcast industry. In 2010 we moved into film with our own cameras and accessories, then built a full post-production studio so productions could finish where they shoot.

Today we run two facilities in Andheri West, opposite Yash Raj Studios, for high-end features and lean budgets alike. We are an empanelled film production house for Indian Oil Corporation.
